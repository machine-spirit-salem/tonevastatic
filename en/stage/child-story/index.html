A Child’s Story

  • Idea and Concept: Elena Dimitrova
  • Stage Adaptation of Text : Elena Dimitrova and Yavor Kostov-Yondin
  • Direction: Elena Dimitrova and Yavor Kostov-Yondin
  • Actor: Yavor Kostov-Yondin
  • Scenography and light design: Ralitza Toneva
  • Composer: Lyubomir Brashnenkov
  • Multimedia: Klara Stoyanova
  • Graphic Design: Kalina Filkova
  • Translation: Mariya Savova
  • Photos: Ivan Donchev, Ralitza Toneva
  • Production: Association “Force Minor”
  • Toplocentrala, Sofia 2024

“A Child’s Story” is part of the “Slow Homecoming” tetralogy by the Nobel Prize in Literature laureate – Peter Handke. In the novella, the author recounts ten years in the life of a man following the birth of his child. He raises his daughter alone after separating from her mother. “A Child’s Story” however, is also a story about loneliness and alienation, the crisis of a family breakdown, the difficulty of fitting in, vulnerability and coping. The performance is an attempt at a theatrical diary, searching for the metaphysics of the trivial when a child appears in the life of an adult.
