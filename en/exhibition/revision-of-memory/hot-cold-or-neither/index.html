Exhibitions / Revision of Memory, 2025

Hot, Cold or Neither

Found glass, silk, processor ventilator, wood, led lights

When glass is broken, its shape becomes irregular and takes it one step back to nature, of which it is a copy. The soft branches and phalanges of the walnut branches imitate the lines of broken glass, or rather the reverse. When does the resemblance appear? The fake silk fire is a naive ancestor of the screen. In the zone of peripheral vision this partial sensory illusion works, despite its poignant deficiency.
